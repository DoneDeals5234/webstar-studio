Citizen Grievance Redressal Desk

Lodge public grievances regarding municipal failures (e.g. road repair, waste management, streetlights). Upon submission, a grievance record is logged in the department database, a receipt is generated, and a direct alert is sent to ward engineers.

⚠️ Filing false complaints is punishable under section 182 of the municipal code.
